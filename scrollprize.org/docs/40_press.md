## About Vesuvius Challenge

Vesuvius Challenge is an open science research organization reading the carbonized Herculaneum scrolls.
Buried by the eruption of Mount Vesuvius in 79 AD, the scrolls are too fragile to open and unroll; we read them instead with high-resolution X-ray CT scans and AI, virtually unwrapping each sheet and recovering its ink.
The work is done both by our in-house team of researchers and by a lively community, competing for monthly prizes anyone can enter.
In 2023, the Grand Prize was awarded for the first passages ever read from inside a sealed scroll; in 2026, PHerc. 1667 became the [first Herculaneum scroll read end to end](/firstscroll).
