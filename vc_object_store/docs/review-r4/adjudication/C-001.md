Disposition: **rejected (proposed trigger not established through supported operations)**. Severity: **none assigned**. Independent source review at `2b5d1e01fd26f27b3410d1b024c3f10a73533287`; no runtime reproduction.

`AttachedDocument.cpp:169–180` sets only attachment kind and applicability, constructs the whole recorded graph, and filters role afterward. `Traversal.cpp:318–368` expands matching relationships recursively and ultimately resolves every selected endpoint in `graphReadViewAt`; `EdgeSql.cpp:49–67` has no role predicate. Thus an unavailable unrelated endpoint would prevent the requested document from being returned. The concrete caller is `CWindow.cpp:2584–2593`, selected Lasagna results loading.

The missing step is a legal way to create that unavailable endpoint while retaining the selected owner's evidence. `ObjectCollection.cpp:375,482` requires referenced dependencies to be sealed. A linked history-off document cannot then be edited in place; an overlay override does not remove its immutable base version. A local history-off store does not simultaneously provide the ordinary retained older owner state assumed by the trigger, and ordinary endpoint updates can rebind the relationship. A mixed-policy hypothetical is therefore insufficient. The discovery author subsequently supplied the same negative check.

The initial adjudication conditionally accepted that hypothetical; this final disposition corrects it after tracing the immutability boundary. Broad traversal may be an API/performance improvement opportunity, but the generic closure is intentionally fail-closed. Reopen only with a supported operation sequence that leaves an unrelated endpoint unavailable or ambiguous while the requested role remains readable in isolation.
