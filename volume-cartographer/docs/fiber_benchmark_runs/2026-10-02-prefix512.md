The crop filter checks each side from its original seed at committed vertices spaced at least512base voxels, plus the terminal endpoint. Crop terminal points preserve existing boundary clipping and can therefore be interpolated. A reverse trace must remain in a20normal/40tangential ellipse until the current prefix start. On rejection, retain only through the last accepted checkpoint, discard the rest through the next vertex, and begin an independent prefix there. Never bridge discarded intervals. Short pieces are not removed. Unlike reviewed reference replay, this does not GT-reseed a new forward path: it salvages suffixes of already generated candidates.
