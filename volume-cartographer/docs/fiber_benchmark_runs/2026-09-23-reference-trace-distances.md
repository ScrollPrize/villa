# Whole-source trace distances to reference fibers

Measured 2026-09-23, new inference beam-8 source traces; no tracing, inference, or BP rerun. Both 1024³ and 2048³ crops are included. Trace IDs are zero-based source-polyline indices in the respective OBJ, not BP pieces, and are not shared across crops.

## Definition

- Reference polylines are clipped by exact segment/box intersections to their crop. Whole source traces remain untrimmed.
- Regular acceptance ellipse: 20 base voxels along sheet normal, 80 in the tangent plane. Local normals use native NormalSampler from the sd2-5b17ff6c Paris4 Lasagna manifest.
- Rank each whole source trace by arc-length-weighted mean reference-to-trace ellipse distance. The ellipse distance is sqrt(d_normal²/20² + d_tangent²/80²); ≤1 is inside. This ranking does not necessarily minimize ordinary Euclidean distance or prefer a complete matching trace.
- Mean and median distances are arc-length-weighted over the reference; median is the weighted 50th percentile. Mean distance (base voxels) is reference-to-nearest-trace-segment Euclidean distance, separately minimized, averaged over reference arc length. Reverse mean is also reported in the CSV.
- Ref covered = reference length whose ellipse intersects that trace. Trace inside = length of the complete trace inside the union of reference-local ellipses. These are distinct denominators.
- Length integration uses midpoint samples spaced ≤2 base voxels, weighted by their actual segment lengths; projection is onto continuous segments, not integer voxels or sampled trace points. For reverse queries, normals are held at reference segment midpoints. Threshold lengths remain numerical approximations, not exact containment guarantees; OBJ coordinate rounding also limits precision.
- No tangent-angle rejection is applied: this answers geometric ellipse containment. The earlier source-trace report also required 25° agreement and used a symmetric 95th-percentile ranking, so its selected trace IDs and percentages may differ. An unrelated crossing can have some coverage without being a corresponding fiber; trace purity exposes this.
- Spatial pruning uses a conservative lower bound (distance to trace bounding box / 80); all traces capable of entering the top three are tested.
- Base voxels convert to mm by ×0.0024.
