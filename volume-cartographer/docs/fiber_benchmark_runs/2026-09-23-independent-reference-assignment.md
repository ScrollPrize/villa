## Protocol

Each reference is ranked independently against its own reference-to-generated constraints; no reference-to-reference optimization, no conditioned BP, no GT-driven piece removal, no fresh pruned solve. Old weights: hard perpendicular signs; no parallel signs; parallel-one magnitude0.1; other magnitude classes0; continuity/defects unchanged. The native observation scorer is shared. No unitization of weights or hard-sign relaxation in objective. Infeasible candidates are diagnostic rankings ordered first by number of violated hard signs, then finite weighted loss. Zero-weight evidence is ignored; inactive endpoints are excluded. Sign violations count observations, not unique neighboring source fibers; BP subdivision may provide several observations.

Global sign/per-gauge offset is fitted against GT solely for output alignment/scoring (2048: sign−1, gauge0 offset2.5). This is not held-out gauge calibration. Half-winding grid, no extra GT-parity filter; ranking is winding-only, not optimization of reference orientation. Search spans all admitted energy/sign breakpoints plus2windings at either end; explicit intervals, ties, and unbounded feasible/optimal tails in logs. All best candidates in these runs are unique by exact configured score. First5alternatives saved; lower rank does NOT mean hard-feasible.
